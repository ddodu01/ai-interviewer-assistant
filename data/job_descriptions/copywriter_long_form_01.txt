Job Title: Copywriter, Long-Form

Company: Carbon
Location: Fully remote, with significant overlap to CET working hours
Work Type: Part-time / Retainer model
Role Type: Copywriting, Long-form content, B2B technology writing

Company Overview:
Carbon builds, operates, and transfers nearshore engineering hubs for global technology companies. Through its Build-Operate-Transfer model, Carbon sets up fully operational satellite offices in strategic European markets, operates them on behalf of clients, and transfers them to client ownership when they are ready to bring the hub in-house.

Carbon partners with venture-backed startups and established multinationals to extend engineering capacity across software, design, product, and AI, without requiring clients to set up an international entity themselves.

Why Carbon:
Carbon believes that strong engineering talent is not limited to the most obvious technology hubs. Some of Europe's strongest technical communities are undervalued, underutilized, and ready to build. Carbon's mission is to connect that talent with companies that need it through hubs that clients can eventually own outright.

About the Role:
Carbon is hiring a part-time Copywriter to write long-form content. The role focuses on articles, essays, and thought-leadership pieces published on Carbon's website. These pieces define how Carbon communicates and argues about its market space.

The copywriter will produce two to four long-form pieces per month. Each piece should carry a clear point of view and demonstrate strong editorial discipline. The writing should be deeper than typical marketing content and should be valuable enough for senior technical buyers, private equity operating partners, and founders to finish reading.

The copywriter will work with Carbon's Strategist and founder to identify worthwhile topics, develop strong arguments, and produce polished long-form content. The role requires the ability to push back on weak briefs, improve unclear arguments, and protect quality standards even when there is pressure to publish.

Reporting Line:
- Reports to the Strategist and the founder.

Responsibilities:
- Write two to four long-form articles per month for Carbon's website.
- Cover topics such as the Build-Operate-Transfer model, nearshore engineering, governance, operational maturity, and structural questions relevant to buyers.

- Help shape an editorial calendar where pieces build on each other.
- Identify arguments that can be repurposed into social posts, sales assets, or longer-form sections.
- Adapt ideas across formats without diluting them.
