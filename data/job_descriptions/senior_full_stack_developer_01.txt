About the Role:
Edgeless is building a global platform that connects independent professionals, suppliers, and clients across onboarding, matching, contracts, time recording, and invoicing. The company is expanding internationally and focuses on AI-augmented software development.

The role involves working as a Senior Full Stack Developer in an engineering environment where AI tools are deeply integrated into the software development lifecycle. Engineers use AI agents to support implementation, while they validate output, enforce architecture principles, ensure security, and maintain code quality.

How We Work:
The company uses Claude Code and Cursor as primary AI-augmented development environments. Features are developed through AI-assisted workflows involving structured documentation, skill containers, and agentic task execution. Engineers define constraints, validate AI-generated output, and enforce quality gates. Code reviews focus on architecture, security, domain correctness, and alignment with engineering principles.
