Job Title: Data Analyst

    Company Context:
    We are a growing technology team building software products for international clients. The role involves collaboration with developers, QA engineers, product owners and stakeholders.

    Role Overview:
    Analyze business data and create dashboards for product and operations teams.

    Responsibilities:
    - Analyze requirements and clarify technical assumptions with the team.
    - Design, implement, test and document reliable solutions.
    - Collaborate during code reviews, planning sessions and demos.
    - Improve maintainability, performance and quality of the product.

    Must Have Requirements:
    - SQL
- Python
- Pandas
- Excel
- Power BI
- Data Visualization
- Communication

    Nice to Have / Good to Have:
    - Tableau
- BigQuery
- Snowflake
- Statistics

    Soft Skills:
    - Communication
    - Teamwork
    - Problem Solving
    - Ownership

    Seniority:
    Open to junior, mid or senior candidates depending on the final profile and project fit.
