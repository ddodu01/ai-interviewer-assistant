Company Overview:
Bending Spoons aims to build one of the all-time great companies by serving a large number of customers, helping team members reach their full potential, operating with high effectiveness and efficiency, creating value for shareholders, and maintaining high ethical standards.

The company acquires and improves digital businesses for long-term ownership and operation. Its teams work on established products with the ambition, agility, and urgency of a startup. Bending Spoons integrates AI deeply into its work so that human judgment and machine intelligence reinforce each other.

About the Role:
The Software Engineer Intern role is aimed at a talented, driven, and collaborative candidate who wants to learn quickly, make an impact, and progress their career. The role involves building real software systems used by millions of people and contributing to products at scale.

- Work on products that evolve and scale over time.
- Use AI tools directly in the development workflow.
- Apply AI tools to design, implementation, testing, and documentation.
- Maintain high standards for correctness, reliability, and maintainability.
- Work across diverse technology stacks with end-to-end ownership.
- Choose appropriate technologies for each engineering challenge.
- Work with monoliths and microservices.
- Work with API technologies such as gRPC and REST.
- Work with infrastructure technologies such as Kubernetes and Docker.
- Work with programming languages such as Python and Rust.
- Apply technologies thoughtfully, focusing on depth and purpose rather than trends.
- Question unnecessary complexity.
- Improve architectures, pipelines, and codebases.
- Build systems that are simpler, more scalable, and easier to maintain.
